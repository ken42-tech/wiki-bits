Datta Meghe Institute of Higher Education & Research (DMIHER) is a Deemed to be University established in 2005 by UGC under Section 3 of UGC Act, 1956. DMIHER is the highest NAAC A++ accredited Health Science University in India with CGPA 3.78 out of 4 in its 4th cycle of accreditation.
**Key Highlights:**
- 270+ acres self-sustained campus
- 700+ full-time teaching faculty
- 150+ multidisciplinary courses
- 9+ hospitals on & off campus
- NAAC A++ accredited (4th cycle) with CGPA 3.78/4
- Highest accredited Health Science University in India
**Vision:**
To build a world class progressive temple of learning conducive to learning excellence, impactful research, community development and value education, creating professionals to shape the world by serving the humanity.
**Mission:**
- To enhance quality teaching learning process and research to provide need based education.
- To continuously update curriculum in consonance with the changing requirements of society and stakeholders.
- To enhance scope of all existing collaborations and associations with the best universities/ institutes nationally and globally.
- To ensure overall personality development with special focus on sports, fine arts, cultural integration and co-curricular activities.
