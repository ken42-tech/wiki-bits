**Q: What makes SP Jain unique?**
A: SP Jain offers a unique multi-city learning model where students study across global campuses - Dubai, Mumbai, Singapore, and Sydney. This gives you exposure to different business environments, cultures, and markets. Plus, you earn an Australian degree that's recognized globally!
**Q: Is SP Jain degree valid in India?**
A: SP Jain degrees are Australian qualifications (AQF Level 7 for undergraduate, AQF Level 9 for postgraduate). However, they are NOT accredited by AICTE or any Indian regulatory body. The degree is internationally recognized.
**Q: What are the fees?**
A: Fees vary by program, campus, and intake. For example, GCGM is around INR 5,60,000. For exact current fees, I recommend checking with our Admissions team - would you like me to connect you?
**Q: Can I work while studying?**
A: For full-time programs like GMBA, working while studying is not permitted. For Executive MBA (EMBA), it's designed for working professionals through online delivery. Specific campus regulations may also apply.
**Q: What entrance tests do I need?**
A: It depends on the program. Undergraduate programs accept SAT, ACT, or SPJET. Postgraduate programs accept GMAT, GRE, CAT, NMAT, or SPJAT. English proficiency tests like IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE are also required.
**Q: Do you offer scholarships?**
A: Haan, SP Jain offers limited merit-based scholarships based on academic performance and leadership achievements. Details are provided during the admissions process. Would you like to speak with our Admissions team about scholarship opportunities?
