Agentic AI horizontal specialisation

Agentic AI prepares learners to design, build, evaluate, secure and operate AI agents that retrieve knowledge, use tools, coordinate tasks and interact with people and enterprise systems under defined controls. The focus extends beyond generating responses to carrying out multi-step tasks with appropriate evaluation, security and human oversight.

Who should consider this specialisation

Working professionals in software engineering, AI and ML, automation, data engineering, analytics, technical product roles and solution architecture may find the specialisation relevant to their goals. Eligible fresh graduates with the required technical preparation, and learners pursuing another postgraduate programme concurrently, may also consider it.

Programming, API integration, data handling and introductory LLM knowledge are recommended capabilities. These recommendations describe preparation for the specialisation; they do not establish a separate admission route or replace the MSc eligibility requirements.

Course pool and completion requirement

The Agentic AI course pool comprises four courses of 3 units each, totalling 12 units. A student must complete at least three courses, earning a minimum of 9 units, to attain this horizontal specialisation. The existing MSc degree structure and specialisation completion framework remain applicable.

 

Mandatory prerequisites

  • Complete MDS DF423 – Generative AI Applications, the mandatory prerequisite for the Agentic AI specialisation.
  • Complete both MDS DG534 – Retrieval-Augmented Generation & Knowledge Systems and MDS DG535 – Advanced Multi-Agent Systems & Orchestration before registering for MDS DG537 – Agentic AI Applications: Cross-Domain Systems.
  • Course selection and registration follow the applicable academic requirements and published offerings.

What the courses cover

  • MDS DG534 – Retrieval-Augmented Generation & Knowledge Systems: Retrieval methods, embeddings, vector search, document processing, hybrid retrieval and re-ranking, structured and graph-based retrieval, evaluation, knowledge freshness and access control. Security topics include knowledge-base poisoning, indirect prompt injection and privacy.
  • MDS DG535 – Advanced Multi-Agent Systems & Orchestration: Task decomposition, planning, agent memory and state, tool use, delegation, coordination, interoperability and human approval mechanisms. Evaluation and agent-specific security risks are integrated into the course.
  • MDS DG536 – LLMOps & Production Agent Engineering: Deployment, inference optimisation, continuous evaluation, tracing, observability, failure analysis and recovery, latency and cost management, model adaptation, guardrails, secure execution and governance.
  • MDS DG537 – Agentic AI Applications: Cross-Domain Systems: Selecting suitable business problems, software-engineering agents, service agents, enterprise workflow automation, retrieval-driven analytics and other applications. The course includes user experience, business viability, organisational rollout and an integrated build sprint with demonstration and defence.

Capabilities learners can develop

  • Decide when a predefined workflow, a single agent or a multi-agent system is appropriate.
  • Build retrieval pipelines and connect AI systems to knowledge sources, tools and services with suitable access controls.
  • Engineer planning, memory, state management, task delegation and human oversight.
  • Evaluate retrieval quality, task completion, agent trajectories and security robustness against documented baselines and test cases.
  • Deploy and monitor systems while managing reliability, latency, resource use and cost.
  • Apply privacy, security, responsible AI and governance practices throughout the agent lifecycle.
  • For learners taking MDS DG537, integrate technical work with user interaction design, an evaluation report, a business case, and security and governance documentation.

Coverage depends on the courses selected. Completion of all four courses provides coverage of the full set of stated outcomes; a three-course selection should not be described as covering every outcome.

Illustrative application

An enterprise service agent could retrieve an approved procedure, consult an authorised business system, recommend a next action and request human approval before a sensitive action. This example illustrates how retrieval, tool use, coordination and oversight fit together; it is not a promised assignment or deployment outcome.

Professional relevance

The capabilities may support work in AI engineering, generative AI application development, software and automation engineering, ML engineering, LLMOps or MLOps, data and analytics engineering, AI evaluation, solution architecture and technical product practice. Relevance depends on the learner’s background, selected courses and demonstrated work. The specialisation does not guarantee a particular role, promotion or salary.

Agentic AI applicant questions and answers

Is Agentic AI a separate programme or part of the MSc?

It is a horizontal specialisation within the MSc in Data Science & AI. It is not a separate degree or a replacement for the programme’s data science and AI foundations.

How is Agentic AI different from a chatbot or a fixed automation workflow?

A conversational application may primarily generate responses, while a fixed workflow follows predefined steps. An agentic system can retrieve information, select and use tools, maintain state and coordinate actions across a multi-step task under defined controls. The specialisation also teaches learners to judge when a simpler workflow is the more dependable solution.

Do I need to complete all four courses?

The minimum horizontal completion requirement is three courses or 9 units from the four-course pool. The full pool carries 12 units. Course choices must satisfy the prerequisites and applicable academic rules.

Can I register for the applications course straight away?

MDS DG537 requires completion of both MDS DG534 and MDS DG535. MDS DF423 – Generative AI Applications is also the mandatory prerequisite for the specialisation. Follow the issued registration instructions rather than assuming a particular trimester sequence.

Does the applications course replace the MSc Capstone?

MDS DG537 includes an integrated build sprint and demonstration/defence. This does not replace the degree’s separately prescribed Capstone requirement. The introduction of Agentic AI retains the existing MSc degree structure and graduation requirements.

Does taking a Mathematics or Programming Refresher satisfy these prerequisites?

The Agentic AI prerequisites are the named MSc courses above. The optional academic refreshers support foundational readiness and should not be presented as substitutes for these course prerequisites.

Is this only suitable for experienced professionals?

Working professionals can connect the learning with their existing technical or business context. Eligible fresh graduates with the required preparation may also consider it. Readiness for programming, APIs, data handling and introductory LLM concepts matters alongside the formal course prerequisites.

Will I receive a guaranteed Agentic AI role?

The specialisation can help learners develop relevant engineering capabilities and practical evidence of their work. A job, promotion or salary increase is not guaranteed. Outcomes depend on experience, individual performance, available opportunities and employer requirements.

M.Sc. in Data Science & AI – Academic Refresher Programmes

Knowledge Base for AI Chatbot, Voice Bot & Admissions Counselors

Overview

BITS Pilani Digital offers two optional academic refresher programmes for eligible applicants to the M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme:

1. Mathematics Refresher Programme

2. Programming Refresher Programme

These programmes are designed to help learners strengthen foundational skills before commencing the M.Sc. programme.

They are not degree programmes, certificate programmes, admission pathways, or bridge programmes.

Important Clarification

The Refresher Programmes are optional academic refresher programmes.

Applicants who meet the eligibility criteria for the M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme and have received an admission offer may choose either of the following:

Join the M.Sc. programme directly, or

Complete one or more Refresher Programmes before joining the next available M.Sc. intake.

The decision rests entirely with the learner.

No.

Yes.

No.
